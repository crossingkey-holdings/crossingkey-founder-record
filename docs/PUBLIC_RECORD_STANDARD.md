# Public Record Standard

The founder record exists to preserve useful chronology without publishing private material or overstating evidence.

## Include

- dated public releases;
- public repositories;
- verified milestones;
- significant changes in technical direction;
- corrections and status changes.

## Exclude

- credentials or secrets;
- customer information;
- private personal records;
- unpublished proprietary source;
- claims that cannot be tied to evidence;
- private creative canon or unrelated personal material.

## Corrections

A material correction should identify what changed and why. Historical entries should not be rewritten solely to make the past appear cleaner or more advanced than it was.
